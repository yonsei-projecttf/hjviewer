You can now delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. Your PCs then leave My PCs, and every device logged in to the account is logged out. An app installed from the App Store updates in the App Store, with the update screen's "Open the App Store" button. The "Advanced" header in Settings is now "Advanced settings". The About links (the privacy policy and the source code) open in the browser outside the app, and the app opens hjviewer:// links only. The app no longer declares the camera and photo permissions (a PC's camera view stays).
