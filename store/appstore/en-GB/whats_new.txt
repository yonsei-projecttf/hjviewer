The key bar of the remote screen has a Backspace key, next to Del in the row that "..." shows: it deletes one character on the PC whatever the on-screen keyboard holds. Korean typed with the Korean keyboard after deleting text that was already on the PC no longer arrives with a "1" in front. Six seconds after a connection's first picture, the app asks the PC for a new picture once, as Refresh in the session menu does, so that a blurry picture from the first moments of a connection is less likely to stay.
